### Textbooks

**1. Digital Design**<br/>
BY: Morris Mano and Michael Ciletti - Chapter 6 (Sequential Logic)<br/>
_Comprehensive coverage of counter circuits, asynchronous and synchronous counters, and modulo-N counters._

**2. Digital Logic and Computer Design**<br/>
BY: Morris Mano - Chapter 5<br/>
_Fundamental concepts of sequential circuits including flip-flops, counters, and state machines._

**3. Digital Electronics: Principles, Devices and Applications**<br/>
BY: Anil K. Maini - Chapter 8<br/>
_Practical approach to sequential circuit design with detailed analysis of counter implementations._

**4. Digital Systems: Principles and Applications**<br/>
BY: Ronald J. Tocci, Neal S. Widmer, and Gregory L. Moss - Chapter 7<br/>
_Excellent coverage of counter types, applications, and troubleshooting techniques._

**5. Fundamentals of Digital Logic with VHDL Design**<br/>
BY: Stephen Brown and Zvonko Vranesic - Chapter 8<br/>
_Modern approach to sequential circuit design including counter synthesis and optimization._
